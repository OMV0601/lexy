# Clocked: 3-minute demo video

## Before you record

- Browser window at 1440×900, zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden, no other tabs.
- Screen recorder: QuickTime (Mac) or OBS. Record the screen first, then add narration over it.
- Clips to record (each plays itself; just press record, open the link, wait):
  1. Landing page: open `/`, wait 4 seconds, then scroll slowly to the dark stat section and stop.
  2. Main demo: open `/check?demo=play`. It runs about 25 seconds, from choosing the city to the claim summary.
  3. Spanish demo: open `/check?demo=play&lang=es`. You only need the first 12 seconds (the typing and the calendar filling).
  4. Citation click: open `/check?demo=rosa`, click the "Cal. Lab. Code §510" chip so the real statute opens in a new tab.

## Tips

- Keep your voice slow over the result screen. The number running up is the moment the judges will remember, so let it finish before you speak again.
- If you run long, cut the Spanish clip to 5 seconds before cutting anything from the result screen.
- End on the result card or the logo, never on a menu or a loading state.
